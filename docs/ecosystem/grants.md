# XPHERE Grant Program

The **XPHERE Grant Program** supports builders, developers, and teams driving innovation across the XPHERE ecosystem — whether you are launching a dApp, building infrastructure, or growing the community.

:::tip Official grant page — [x-phere.com/grant.html](https://x-phere.com/grant.html)
The page linked above is the authoritative source for the program. This document summarises it for builders already working in the docs.
:::

## What the Program Supports

The program is directed at work that advances the XPHERE ecosystem, across three broad areas:

| Area | Examples of the kind of work |
|------|------------------------------|
| **dApps** | Applications launching on XPHERE |
| **Infrastructure** | Tooling, services, and systems the ecosystem depends on |
| **Community** | Initiatives that grow and support the XPHERE community |

These are the focus areas the Foundation has published. They describe direction, not a closed list.

## How Review Works

Applications are reviewed on a **rolling basis** — there is no application window to wait for, and no published deadline. Submit when your proposal is ready.

**Selected applicants are contacted within 14 business days.** Plan around that timeline rather than an expected decision on a specific date.

:::caution Not published
The Foundation has **not published** grant amounts, funding tiers, milestone structures, or evaluation scoring for this program. Treat any such figures found elsewhere as unofficial. Submitting an application is not a commitment of funding by the Foundation.
:::

## How to Apply

1. Read the official program page at **[x-phere.com/grant.html](https://x-phere.com/grant.html)**.
2. Prepare your proposal (see [Preparing Your Application](#preparing-your-application) below).
3. Submit the **[application form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScRmVenQeBxK9FM_2Y8Ib1pvDx79ohYLBpjDj_rWKBP8PfPcQ/viewform)**.
4. Wait for contact. Selected applicants are contacted within 14 business days.

## Preparing Your Application

:::note These are suggestions, not requirements
The Foundation has not published application requirements or evaluation criteria. The points below are practical guidance for writing a clear submission — nothing here is an official condition of eligibility, and meeting these points does not affect eligibility one way or the other.
:::

A submission is easier to assess when it answers four questions plainly:

| Question | What helps |
|----------|------------|
| **What are you building?** | A clear, concrete description in plain language — what it does and who uses it. |
| **How does it use XPHERE?** | Which parts run on XPHERE, and why the network is the right place for them. |
| **Who is the team?** | Who is doing the work, and relevant prior work or repositories. |
| **What is the current status?** | Idea, prototype, testnet deployment, or live — and what is next. |

Links that let a reviewer verify the work themselves are generally more useful than description alone — a repository, or a contract address on the explorer for the network it is deployed to: [xpscan.io](https://xpscan.io) or [xp.tamsa.io](https://xp.tamsa.io) for Mainnet, [xpt.tamsa.io](https://xpt.tamsa.io) for a Testnet deployment.
